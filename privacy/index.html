Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026. Faker Hunt: Secret Word Party ("the app") is published by Xprin Technology ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information.

What stays on your device

Player names you type, your custom word lists, settings, game progress, statistics and your saved game are stored only on your device. The app has no account system and no server of ours. We cannot see this data and it is never uploaded by us. Custom word lists never leave your phone.

Information collected by third-party services

The app is free and ad-supported. It uses the following Google services, which may collect and process data as described in Google's own policies:

  • Google AdMob (advertising). The app shows ads: a banner on the setup screen, interstitial ads between rounds, optional rewarded video ads (for example to unlock word packs for 24 hours or get an extra hint) and a native ad in the category list. AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), device and app information and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads, and may use this for personalised ads where permitted. Ads are never shown while a secret word is revealed, during clues, voting or a running timer.
  • Firebase Analytics. Collects app interactions (for example the number of players, the chosen category and mode, rounds started and finished, whether the faker was caught, whether you shared a card) and device and diagnostic information, to understand and improve the app. It also records ad revenue events.
  • Firebase Crashlytics. Collects crash reports and diagnostics (device model, OS version, app state at the time of a crash).
  • Firebase Remote Config. Downloads configuration values, such as how often ads are shown, using an installation identifier.
  • Google User Messaging Platform. Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) the app asks for your consent before ads or analytics identifiers are used. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy settings.

Advertising choices

You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads). The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children.

Sharing images

If you tap "Share", the app creates a picture of the Faker Reveal card and hands it to the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes; the app does not upload it anywhere itself.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell your personal information and do not share it with anyone except the service providers named above, acting on our behalf. Data sent by these services is encrypted in transit.

Retention and deletion

Local data is removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Analytics and crash data held by Google is retained according to Google's Firebase retention settings. The app has no accounts, so there is no account to delete. To ask about data connected to your device, contact us.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including the EEA, UK, US states such as California, Canada and Australia) you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information and to withdraw consent. As we do not hold your personal information ourselves, requests relating to Google-held data are best made through your Google account privacy controls or by contacting us.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above.
