Faker Hunt

Free on Google Play

Secret word party game for 3 to 20 players, one phone, no internet needed to play.

Everyone secretly sees the same word, except the faker, who has to bluff. Give one-word clues, spot who is pretending, vote, and watch the dramatic Faker Reveal. Fakers who get caught still get one guess at the word to steal the win.

  • Classic, Two Fakers, Different Question and Custom modes
  • 40+ original word packs including Thanksgiving, Christmas, Halloween, Valentine's Day and more
  • Make your own word lists (they stay on your phone)
  • Hold-to-reveal with auto-hide so nobody can peek
  • Shareable "Faker Reveal" card
  • For ages 13 and up
